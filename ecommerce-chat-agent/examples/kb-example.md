# Example knowledge base content (fictional)

Short, self-contained passages work best with the retrieval tool. Start each passage with the product name and the topic so that keyword queries such as "Product A price" match it. Ingest your own passages into the pgvector table with embeddings from `nomic-embed-text`.

## Product A, price
Product A is sold at 49.00 USD. Taxes are calculated at checkout.

## Product A, warranty
Product A is covered by a [X]-month warranty against manufacturing defects, in addition to any statutory rights that apply in the customer's country. Contact support with the order number to start a claim.

## Product A, usage
Follow the setup steps in the quick-start guide before first use. Full instructions: https://www.example.com/pages/product-a-usage

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes [X] to [Y] business days. A tracking link is sent by email when the order ships.

## Returns
Items can be returned within [X] days of delivery under the conditions described on https://www.example.com/pages/returns. Statutory rights that apply in the customer's country are not affected.

## Product A, troubleshooting
Check the setup steps in the instructions and try again. If the problem persists, contact support with the order number and a short description.

## Subscriptions
Subscriptions are managed in the subscriber area: log in with the email used for the order, then change, pause or cancel the plan.
